# Several outputs on one basis (E34)

> K continuous outputs per case that share one structure, with their K × K noise covariance Σ integrated out: one fit selects terms for every output at once and reads their residual correlation. Built slice by slice from [the brief](../../../issues/regression/bass/multi-output-brief.md), which holds the decisions; [E34's entry](../../../issues/regression/bass/extensions-brief.md#several-outputs-on-one-basis-with-σ-integrated-e34) holds the model's place among the extensions. This page extends BASS's collapsed core ([BayesianRegression.md](./Regression.md)) and does not repeat it. Ordered outputs on a grid stay with the direct and PCA modes.

## The model and its collapse (decision 1; slice 1)

Y (N × K) = 1μᵀ + Z_cA + E, each row of E ∼ N(0, Σ), with the intercept μ ∈ ℝᴷ placed by BASS's convention per output (D03).
- *The priors.* A | Σ, τ ∼ MN(0, (τG_c)⁻¹, Σ) on the centred columns, and Σ ∼ IW(ν₀, Ψ₀) in the conventional degrees of freedom. vec stacks A's columns, one per output, so Cov(vec A) = Σ ⊗ (τG_c)⁻¹. Inclusion is shared across the outputs: the structure is one (decision 4).
- *The collapse.* With A, the intercept and Σ integrated against the likelihood raised to b, the structure step reads the K × K scale S = b(R + τ/(τ + b)·(F + N(ȳ − ℓ)(ȳ − ℓ)ᵀ)) and K times the scalar determinant term, where R = Σᵢ rᵢrᵢᵀ and F = B_cᵀG_c⁻¹B_c are the centred least-squares fit's residual and fitted forms (the scalar ‖r‖² and Q_c carried to K outputs) and ℓ the intercept's centre levels, so the last term is the zero centre's Nȳȳᵀ and none under level centring. S is a sum of semidefinite terms, so a small τ on a close fit keeps its digits as the scalar S does. Σ | Y ∼ IW(ν₀ + ν, Ψ₀ + S), and
  ln p_b(Y | structure, τ) = −(νK/2) ln π + K·(scalar determinant term) + (ν₀/2) ln|Ψ₀| − ln Γ_K(ν₀/2) + ln Γ_K((ν₀ + ν)/2) − ((ν₀ + ν)/2) ln|Ψ₀ + S|,
  exactly 0 at b = 0 under a proper prior. Untempered it is the matrix-t marginal, Brown, Vannucci and Fearn's (1998) eqs. (19)–(21); at K = 1 it is the scalar block's marginal under IG(ν₀/2, Ψ₀/2) (`OutputMarginal`).
- *The count* ν is Nb under level centring and the zero centre, whose intercept sits inside the prior, and Nb − 1 under the flat intercept, whose integral spends an observation. *Corrected 2026-11-08* from "n − 1" in the brief's first draft, at slice 0: the K = 1 reduction to scalar BASS requires Nb, and Brown, Vannucci and Fearn's vague intercept, α ∼ N(α₀, hΣ) with h → ∞, counts N too, its |Σ|^(−½) cancelling in α's integral. A count b(N − 1) would be a third path, the likelihood of Y projected off 1 tempered, which is neither BASS's integrated form nor equal to scalar BASS at K = 1.
- *Alternatives.* Separate scalar fits per output read no residual correlation and select K structures. Inclusion per output needs an indicator per (term, output) and loses the matrix-t (decision 4, not built). The response envelope and an output basis from E[J W_x Jᵀ] are E34's later options.

## Σ's prior (decision 2; slice 1)

**Decision 2, the default:** ν₀ = K − 1 + 2c and Ψ₀ = 2c·S_Y, c = 10⁻³, stated as an empirical prior. S_Y is scalar BASS's s² carried to K outputs: the outputs' mean squares and cross-products about the intercept's centre levels with divisor N, each entry by s²'s own arithmetic (ȳ under level centring and the flat intercept, 0 under the zero centre; *corrected 2026-11-08* from "sample covariance about their means").
- *At K = 1* it is IG(c, c·s²), the scalar default: S_Y is s² to the bit, and the resolved prior's density is the inverse gamma's.
- *Units.* S_Y → LᵀS_YL when Y → YL, so ln p(YL) = ln p(Y) − (Nb − f) ln|det L| at every structure and τ, f = 1 under the flat intercept: the structure and τ posteriors are invariant to any invertible rescaling or mixing of the outputs.
- *Its correlations.* Under a diagonal S_Y each prior correlation has density ∝ (1 − ρ²)^(c − 1): Huang and Wand's (2013) Property 3 at their ν = 2c, read through a correlation's invariance to a diagonal rescaling. At c = 10⁻³ nearly all of its mass sits at ±1, Gelman's (2006) objection to IG(ε, ε) carried to correlations. The data overrule it at moderate N/K; with few cases per output they may not. Slice 3's fixed-structure comparison at N/K from 3 to 30 finds better small-sample correlation coverage with Huang and Wand's prior at ν = 2, but mixed joint-predictive results and different interval widths. The [report](../../../issues/regression/bass/multi-output-brief.md#slice-3-joint-readouts-and-covariance-prior-coverage-chip-2026-11-09) records the uncertainty and the scalar-reduction and output-mixing tradeoffs. The default remains unchanged pending the user's decision.
- *Refused:* an output with no spread about its level, as scalar BASS refuses a constant response; and under this form, outputs whose S_Y is singular or nearly so, read on the correlation scale as a Cholesky pivot 1 − R² under 10⁻¹⁰. Collinear outputs need fewer outputs.

**The options** (`BassConfig.OutputCovariance`, `OutputCovariancePrior`; null carries `Sigma2`'s ScaledToResponse(c), and Sigma2's other kinds are refused with the reason):
- *Standardized(c):* Ψ₀ = 2c·diag S_Y, the default on outputs standardized to unit mean square: invariant to rescaling the outputs, not to mixing them, and centred on uncorrelated outputs. Collinear outputs pass.
- *A declared IW(ν₀, Ψ₀)* in the outputs' own units: free of the data, as Geweke's test needs.
- *Huang and Wand's prior* (`OutputCovariancePrior.HuangWand`, slice 2) uses Σ | a ∼ IW(ν + K − 1, 2ν diag(1/a)), a_k ∼ IG(½, 1/A_k²). A_k defaults to each output's root mean square about its level. Explicit positive scales declare the prior independently of the responses. The default ν = 2 gives uniform correlations, and σ_k has the half-t(ν, A_k) marginal. At K = 1 it is the engine's hierarchical σ² prior at hyper-shape ½ (`Sigma2Prior.Hierarchical(ν/2, ½)`, A = s).

The sampler carries r_k = ν/a_k on v_k = ln r_k. Its hyperprior is Gamma(½, rate 1/(νA_k²)) and its conditional covariance prior is IW(ν + K − 1, 2 diag r). The matrix-t collapse holds at fixed r. Each rate takes a slice step against its replica's own target, including the log-coordinate Jacobian in the hyperprior. Exact reference draws sample the rates independently of the admitted structure. Replicas, particles, checkpoints and retained draws carry the rates. Choosing this option is independent of the default-prior coverage comparison commissioned for slice 3.

## Properness under tempering (decision 3)

At rung b, Σ's conditional is IW(ν₀ + ν_b, Ψ₀ + S_b), proper where ν₀ + ν_b > K − 1. With the intercept inside a proper prior that holds at every b ≥ 0, the reference rung included. Under the flat intercept it fails at Nb ≤ K − ν₀ under a proper prior too (Nb ≤ 1 − 2c under decision 2), and b = 0 diverges, as scalar BASS's flat pole does (`BassTarget.Defines`). Resolution checks this condition for the ladder's hottest rung and for any requested reference rung. Integrated SMC needs the b = 0 member. The linear form reads b·ln p₁ and reaches b = 0 with a proper structure/scale prior. A flat intercept's unspecified prior constant still leaves absolute evidence unavailable.

The entry's first condition, ν₀ − N(1 − b) > K − 1, asked the tilted prior IW(ν₀ + N(b − 1)) alone to be proper, which integrating Σ against that tilted prior needs but integrating the tempered likelihood directly does not.

## Coefficient forms and samplers (slice 2)

The normalized ridge and KHAOS's raw ridge and modified g-prior use the same prior-precision builders as scalar BASS. On the centred design coordinates, let A be the likelihood Gram and D the prior precision per unit τ. Factoring D = LLᵀ and L⁻¹AL⁻ᵀ = VΛVᵀ gives one generalized spectrum per structure. Each output contributes its own cross products w_k = VᵀL⁻¹c_k. The `OutputPencil` shares the eigenvectors and keeps these K projections through `PriorPencil.WithCross`.

With H_j,kl = w_jk w_jl / λ_j, the integrated scale is

`S_b = bR + Σ_j [bτ/(τ + bλ_j)] H_j`.

Its determinant term is `(K/2) Σ_j ln[τ/(τ + bλ_j)]`, plus `−(K/2) ln(Nb)` for the flat intercept. The eigenbasis includes the intercept when its prior is proper. This representation keeps the residual form explicit at small τ. The current implementation uses a fresh full-spectrum decomposition for each proposed structure, O(M³), followed by O(KM²) for the K cross-product projections. Acceptance retains the already-scored immutable pencil and its projections. A repeated temperature or τ evaluation builds the shrinkage form in O(MK²), then factors the K × K posterior scale in O(K³) for its log determinant; the analytic slope also uses K SPD solves. These costs are separate from the design's Gram, cross products and explicit residuals. The g-form retains its closed form, and the scalar ridge retains its bordered spectrum. The posterior matrix-normal draw uses the same pencil's precision and K means, then mixes independent coefficient innovations across the outputs through Σ's Cholesky factor.

Plain chains, the parallel-tempering ladder, SMC and the flat histogram consume these target values through their existing sampler interfaces. The ladder and SMC evidence use normalized prior draws on the admissible structure space F. SMC's stage-budget, collapse and between-replicate flags retain their existing meanings. The histogram samples the joint state with its learned weight as a function of the conditional collapsed log likelihood, then reweights to b = 1. Their monitored predictive functionals are ln|Σ| and the fitted value at each (case, output) point. Evidence metadata records the declared covariance prior and the resolved output scales.

For the integrated form, let T = Ψ₀ + S_b and t = ν₀ + Nb − f. Its derivative is

`d ln p_b/db = d(det term)/db + (N/2)[Σ_i ψ((t−i)/2) − K ln π − ln|T|] − (t/2) tr(T⁻¹ dS_b/db)`,

where i runs from 0 to K−1. For the pencil, `dS_b/db = R + Σ_j [τ/(τ+bλ_j)]² H_j`. The implementation evaluates the trace by SPD solves. The linear form's derivative is the untempered log marginal.

## The design's K-output side (slice 1)

A design of several outputs keeps the scalar design's structure, factor, admission and gate, which read no response, and holds the response's statistics in a K-output side (`DesignOutputs`) in place of the scalar ones: the outputs centred on their means, each term's K cross products, β̂ (M × K), the explicit residuals and the forms R and F.
- *Proposals* carry the scalar bordered updates to K × K. A birth or a change reads R′_kl = R̃_kl − z̃_k z̃_l/(newest·δ) on the reduced model's R̃ and z̃, a change's gain β̂_mβ̂_mᵀ/(G⁻¹)_mm and β̂_m·uᵀG⁻¹e_m/(G⁻¹)_mm; a death's R′ = R + β̂_mβ̂_mᵀ/(G⁻¹)_mm. Where a move explains nearly all of one output's residual the candidate's residuals are formed explicitly, as the scalar design forms its own. F′ is read from the candidate factor's whitening of each output's crosses, F′_kl = (L′⁻¹b′_k)·(L′⁻¹b′_l) (`BorderedCholesky.ProposedWhiten`, beside `ProposedInverseQuadraticForm`).
- *Acceptances* solve β̂ and recompute every residual explicitly, so nothing drifts; snapshots carry the side for a restart bit for bit.
- *Scalar BASS is unchanged by construction:* every K-output path is gated on the side, and the pinned tier passes at each commit.
- *What it costs:* a proposal adds O(NK) for the crosses and O(M²K + MK²) for F′ over the scalar step, an acceptance O(NMK) for the residuals; the hinge prefix tables stand aside for several outputs in slice 1, since their borders are approximate and the K-output side reads exact ones. The cost row is below.

## The draw (slice 1)

A retained draw is exact given (structure, τ): Σ | Y from its inverse Wishart by Bartlett's decomposition through Ψ's own factor (`InverseWishart`), then A | Σ ∼ MN(β̂/(τ + 1), (τ + 1)⁻¹G_c⁻¹, Σ), and the intercepts α | Σ ∼ N(m, Σ·c₀/N) apart from them: m = ℓ + (ȳ − ℓ)/(τ + 1) and c₀ = 1/(τ + 1) inside the prior, m = ȳ and c₀ = 1 under the flat intercept (`BassOutputDraws`). The stream is read as the scalar draw reads it, Σ's gamma variate and normals, then each output's M normals through the factor's innovation, mixed across the outputs by Σ's factor, then the intercepts' K normals, so at K = 1 a fit is scalar BASS's draw for draw on one seed. `BassDraw` holds Σ and the coefficients as one row per term and one column per output (`OutputCoefficients`), uncentred in the ids' order.

## The run (slice 1)

A run of several outputs monitors ln|Σ| in σ²'s place, and each monitored case's fitted value at every output: the monitored points are (case, output) pairs, a declared case without an output filling in every output. ln|Σ| reads the correlations as well as the scales, and its rank-normalized R̂ is moved by no rescaling or mixing of the outputs. The alternative, Σ's whole lower triangle, reads each entry apart at the cost of K(K + 1)/2 functionals and another shape for the diagnostics; it is a follow-up if a run's correlations converge more slowly than ln|Σ| shows.

## Joint prediction and output functionals (slice 3)

`BassMultiOutput.Predict(fit, data, cases, options)` reads the retained A and Σ. Its arrays are draw × case × named output, and its weights follow the requested pooled draw selection, repeats included. At each case it draws one correlated Gaussian vector through Σ's factor; noises at different cases are independent given the retained parameters. `Quantile` integrates that Gaussian noise analytically per output. The predictive covariance is Cov(f) + E[Σ]; the latent covariance is Cov(f). The corresponding correlations use the joint covariance. `Covariance` separately reports the noise-covariance entries and correlations, keeping correlation(E[Σ]) apart from E[correlation(Σ)]. Where the posterior inverse-Wishart degree ν is at most K + 1, E[Σ] is absent and the predictive covariance readout refuses the nonexistent moment. Entry quantiles and joint draws remain available.

`JointBands` uses the weighted quantile of the maximum standardized deviation of the joint draws. By default the maximum ranges over outputs at each case; `AcrossCases` takes one maximum over the whole requested case block. The standardizers are empirical draw moments, so this is a definition of a simultaneous posterior region, not a frequentist coverage guarantee. `SpecificationProbability` counts the weighted joint event that every output of one case lies inside its declared bounds. Both are Monte Carlo views over the stored predictive draws; they do not multiply marginal probabilities or marginal intervals. Their precision depends on the retained posterior draws as well as on the predictive noise draws.

`BassMultiOutput.Conditionals` instead integrates A and Σ given each retained (structure, τ, r), producing a weighted mixture of `MatrixTPredictive` components. For posterior coefficient precision P and mean Ā, new centred design rows X_* give M_* = X_*Ā and V_* = I + X_*P⁻¹X_*ᵀ. Under the flat intercept, add ȳ to M_* and 11ᵀ/N to V_*, and centre the new rows on the training column means. The g-form separates the intercept from the centred coefficients; the generalized pencil carries it inside P under a proper intercept prior. Each component is

`Y_* | Σ ~ MN(M_*, V_*, Σ),   Σ | Y, structure, τ, r ~ IW(ν, Ψ)`.

Its joint log density is

`ln Γ_K((ν+q)/2) − ln Γ_K(ν/2) − (qK/2) ln π − (K/2) ln|V_*| + (ν/2) ln|Ψ| − ((ν+q)/2) ln|Ψ + (Y_*−M_*)ᵀV_*⁻¹(Y_*−M_*)|`,

where q is the requested case count. One output-vector marginal has Student-t degrees ν − K + 1, with scale V_*ii Ψ/(ν − K + 1). Cov(Y_ia,Y_jb) is V_*ij Ψ_ab/(ν − K − 1), where it exists. Joint samples share both this row covariance and one Σ draw. The implementation forms the residual Gram after a triangular solve. `Conditionals.Mean` is the Rao–Blackwellized surface, also available through `Predict`'s `MeanSurface` option.

The conditional readout keeps a q × q row scale per retained state and factors it in O(q³); its density then costs O(q²K + qK² + K³) per state. It is intended for modest joint case blocks. Retained prediction draws avoid those q × q matrices. Structure designs are cached within a conditional-readout call, and all readouts enforce the fit's data fingerprint and completed-posterior condition.

`BassMultiOutput.Sensitivity` takes one declared vector a_f over the named outputs and decomposes a_fᵀf, with coefficients A a_f on the same retained structures. A coordinate vector selects one output. The existing moment-service Sobol kernel supplies the product-measure decomposition, including the original draw weights. `Gradient` reports the K derivatives in the inputs' raw units. `CoActive` takes two output functionals through E8's `CoActiveSpec.Output` and `OtherOutput`, pairing each draw with itself. It reuses E8's exact moments, sampled gradients, input metrics and matrix summaries. The joint draw weighs once; pairing two independently sampled scalar posteriors would be a different distribution.

The covariance-prior coverage comparison is a separate fixed-structure control. Both priors use the same datasets and future observations, a mean-centred intercept structure, held τ = 1/N and empirical output scales. Its uncertainty is over independent datasets, with paired prior differences and independent-chain sensitivity checks for the Monte Carlo intervals and bands. The [slice 3 report](../../../issues/regression/bass/multi-output-brief.md) states the measurements and separates this control from the nonlinear fitted-structure check. The default remains the user's decision after these measurements.

## Notation

Σ is the K × K noise covariance and ν, ν₀ the inverse Wishart's conventional degrees of freedom; Brown, Vannucci and Fearn write IW(δ; Q) in Dawid's shape, δ = ν − K + 1, and their q is this page's K. R and F are the K × K forms of the scalar ‖r‖² and Q_c, and S the K × K scale of the scalar S. Where K, F, R, S_Y, ν, the covariance rates rₖ, marginal scales Aₖ and the pencil's Gram A cross the module's own names is logged in [the notation ledger](../../../issues/notation-ledger.md) (2026-11-09), together with the predictive marginal's ν − K + 1 degrees, case-row scale V_* and case count q.
