## What HOPE actually does

It lifts each neuron out of parameter space into function space: a neuron is the rank-1 Hilbert–Schmidt operator `f = g ⊗ w_out` living in `L²(X, P_X; ℝᶜ) ≅ H_in ⊗ H_out`, where `g(x) = ReLU(w_eff·x + b)`. The measure `P_X` is a MaxEnt Gaussian surrogate reconstructed from the checkpoint's BatchNorm statistics — so every inner product evaluates analytically, with zero data. Capacity = HS norm; redundancy = kernel cosine; pruning = projection to the null operator; merging = the constrained best rank-1 approximation of a rank-2 span (Eckart–Young with a physical-realizability constraint). A layer-level cost `J` is derived from three axioms (scale invariance, a divergence barrier against layer extinction, path-additive relative capacity drain), then a greedy rate–distortion loop picks `argmin J/ΔP` — continuous-knapsack/Dantzig, receding horizon — with residual-block eviction competing in the same currency. The application half (DEFT) uses the capacity ranking to partition core from slack for forgetting-free transfer.

Two deflationary notes, since nobody else will give them to you: the closed-form ReLU kernels are the **Cho–Saul arc-cosine / NNGP machinery** — established infinite-width theory the paper only glancingly credits — and the merge derivation, after its linearization `k(ρ) ≈ ρk(1)`, collapses to the principal eigenvector of a sum of two outer products, i.e. close to what parameter-space "zipping" merges already compute; the genuinely careful parts are the kernel-weighted phase check, the BN-parameter recovery, and putting prune/merge/evict under one normalized cost. And the evaluation is proof-of-concept against weak magnitude baselines, which the authors admit. The framework is the contribution, not the numbers.

## Against the north star

What makes this paper worth your time is not its compression results — it's that it independently converges on **your instrument stack, applied to networks-as-data**:

**The metric–measure factorization is its load-bearing wall.** All the geometry — capacity, similarity, the merge target — is conditioned on a _declared measure_ over activations, and separately the framework quotients out gauge (BN normalization invariance, PH-1 rescaling, resharding) _before_ measuring. That's exactly your gauge-vs-measure split from the coupling-normalization arc, and your "the measure choice silently poisons everything downstream" watch-item applies verbatim: the Gaussian surrogate is defensible for high-fan-in vision nets via CLT, and is precisely the joint that breaks for LLM internals — heavy-tailed, outlier-feature-dominated activation distributions, and no BN to read statistics from (LayerNorm kills the data-free pillar). In your grammar, the MaxEnt surrogate is a _low fidelity rung_, with empirical/moment-matched measures above it. HOPE never says this; your vocabulary does.

**The four-kinds algebra absorbs it without strain.** Surrogate `P_X` = Model. Prune/merge projections = Transforms. Capacity `‖f‖_H` = Observable (a 0-form reduction over the neuron domain). The greedy loop = Inference (reduce). And the compression _trajectory_ — "any intermediate iteration is a valid model" — is a field over the compression-step domain, same shape as your T-sweep. That an external artifact slots into the table this cleanly is real evidence the vocabulary's domain-polymorphism claim wasn't just internal coherence.

**There's a TDA-shaped reading the authors don't make.** Progressive capacity-ordered removal _is a filtration_ on the feature set; "core invariants resist pruning longer than slack" is a persistence statement — core features are the long bars, and DEFT's core/slack threshold is a barcode cut. This is kin to your "energy-landscape PH is the cleaner beachhead than embedding-cloud PH" instinct: here too the filtration parameter is handed to you explicitly rather than guessed from a point cloud. And the merge operation itself is agglomerative clustering of neurons under a kernel-induced metric with a rate–distortion stopping rule — your merge-by-genuine-shared-structure algebra on the neuron domain, plus the Ouroboros reflexivity of feeding a model's own components through clustering machinery.
