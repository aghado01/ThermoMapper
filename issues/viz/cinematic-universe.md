# ThermoMapper cinematic universe

The cinematic universe is an authoring and prioritization device, not a runtime
namespace. Each film asks one scientific question, executes real producers, and
can be entered as a demonstration, opened for diagnosis, or forked for
exploration. Shared artifacts and view grammar make the collection coherent.

## Recurring cast

- **Observed world:** samples and measurements admissible to a canonical method.
- **Construction:** graph or complex built from those observations.
- **Fields:** distances, couplings, affinities, alignments, scalar landscapes,
  tangent or spectral directions.
- **Structure:** assignments, hierarchies, Mapper nerves, bars, generators, and
  lineage.
- **Oracle:** privileged fixture anatomy or external reference, disclosed and
  isolated.
- **Camera:** a view onto artifacts, never a source of scientific state.

## Volume I — Worlds we can know completely

Synthetic generators establish the visual and epistemic grammar because their
latent causes can be inspected without contaminating the canonical method.

### EyeTorus: The crime of proximity

The pilot. A known intrinsic eye is observed as a noisy toroidal point cloud.
Graph construction, coupling, empirical flow, curvature, ambient dimension, and
SPC can each be varied while oracle anatomy remains available for diagnosis.

Primary questions:

- When does ambient proximity create an intrinsically false bridge?
- Which changes come from adjacency, metric, and coupling respectively?
- Does an empirical field follow the generating stroke?
- What survives Euclidean, Poincaré, flattened, and 4D realizations?

### Crescent and Möbius: When local direction lies

Use the existing non-convex fixtures to compare local tangent and spectral line
fields, especially near intersections, seams, twists, and false bridges. Oracle
geometry validates fixture tests; demo takes first show the independent empirical
producer and reveal the oracle only afterward.

### Hyperbolic hierarchy: Distance has a geometry

Place a hierarchical synthetic world in the Poincaré ball, compare Euclidean and
Poincaré graph constructions, and show native geodesics and radial distortion.
The film establishes why coordinates inside a unit ball do not by themselves
make a visualization geometrically honest.

### Simplex and probability worlds: Coordinates are not neutral

Show why compositional/probability data requires an appropriate geometry and why
a naïve Euclidean projection may change neighborhoods. This film drives simplex
coordinate-space and alternate metric support.

## Volume II — Graph laboratories

These films hold samples fixed and treat graph construction as the object of
study.

### A neighborhood is a hypothesis

Compare kNN, mutual-kNN, radius, connectivity repair, and later construction
policies. Show edge births/deaths, components, degree distribution, neighborhood
scales, hubness, MST bridges, and algebraic connectivity.

### Weight of an edge

Freeze adjacency and compare distances, bandwidths, kernels, and edge-weight
semantics. A probe follows one edge from metric distance through kernel coupling
and into downstream consumers.

### The repaired bridge

Compare pre-repair and post-repair graphs while retaining repair provenance.
Demonstrate the difference between combinatorial connectivity and a graph whose
repair edge has negligible effective weight.

## Volume III — Fields and flow

### The directions hidden in a cloud

Compare local tangent, graph-spectral, and other independently produced line or
vector fields on controlled and unknown data. Linked diagnostics expose angular
agreement, degeneracy, component behavior, and uncertainty.

### From edge currency to landscape

Follow affinity or alignment fields through node projections and landscape
construction. Make the modeling choice visible when an edge field becomes a node
height or a basin resolver.

## Volume IV — Thermal stories

### Heating the eye

Run a pinned SPC sweep on EyeTorus. Coordinate temperature with assignments,
affinities, alignments, susceptibility, cluster counts, hierarchy, and lineage.
Oracle hierarchy scores the result without selecting it.

### Domany anchors

Reproduce the BWD/Blatt and Iris reference behaviors as external calibration.
These films emphasize fidelity and provenance over visual novelty, establishing
that later extensions do not break the classical cases.

### One sweep, many scales

Use hierarchical fixtures to show dense structures ordering at different
temperatures, multiple transitions, peripheral capture, and alternative
resolution policies without pretending the sweep is a recursive clusterer.

## Volume V — Shape and topology

### A barcode has a body

Link persistence bars to representative cycles or cocycles and then to their
spatial simplices. Selecting either representation reveals the other and its
filtration birth/death provenance.

### Mapper is a relation, not a second point cloud

Show filter values, cover elements, local clusters, member samples, the nerve,
and Mapper diagnostics as linked artifacts. Mapper nodes retain membership
relations instead of being flattened into anonymous display points.

### Nerves in motion

Use nerve filtrations and diffs to show node matching, births, deaths, edge
changes, and component events over a scientific axis. This drives genuine
multi-artifact temporal synchronization.

### Zigzag: memory in both directions

When the zigzag stack is ready for visualization, show additions and removals
without forcing them into an ordinary monotone-filtration metaphor.

## Volume VI — Statistical landscapes

### Inside a mixture

Show GMM components, covariance geometry, responsibilities, likelihood or BIC
sweeps, and merge strategies. Compare the fitted model with an oracle-generated
mixture only through explicit evidence roles.

### Density makes a tree

Connect HDBSCAN membership probabilities, dendrograms, condensed trees,
outliers, and spatial selections. Contrast its hierarchy with other resolvers
over a shared field where scientifically meaningful.

## Crossovers

Crossovers are study branches sharing upstream artifacts, not giant films that
attempt to explain the whole repository at once. Examples:

- one graph branches to SPC, Mapper, PH, and spectral analysis;
- one EyeTorus latent sample branches to Euclidean and Poincaré realizations;
- one edge field branches to threshold closure, hierarchy, and landscape-based
  resolution;
- one selection links samples, graph edges, Mapper memberships, bars, and
  clusters.

Every crossover must state what is held fixed, what is recomputed, and which
cross-branch relations are exact rather than inferred.

## Production order

1. EyeTorus graph laboratory — establishes entities, coordinates, graphs,
   provenance, A/B panels, selection, oracle overlays, and native geometry.
2. Heating the eye — establishes scientific-axis synchronization and SPC series.
3. Barcode has a body — establishes spatial/non-spatial linked representations.
4. Mapper is a relation — establishes many-to-many relations and nerve panels.
5. Statistical landscape film — validates that the carrier grammar generalizes
   beyond graphs and topology.

The remaining slate is a design reservoir. A film enters production only when
its producers and scientific question are mature enough to constrain the engine.
