# Slice 1 — a held-back spectral band on a new conversation

**Claim to prove.** Fix a predictor fitted on separate training conversations, including its posterior draws and preprocessing. Let G calibration conversations and one new conversation be exchangeable conditional on that fit. Their lengths and contents travel together under permutations. Take conversations with at least one evaluated turn. For each turn apply the same masking rule to obtain visible features X and a held-back vector Y on a fixed nonempty finite bin set. The predictor sees X. The waveform supplies Y for scoring. If masks or turn choices are random, include their independent, identically generated marks in the exchangeable units.

1. **Define acceptance in physical units.** For a turn use the nonconformity score s = max over bins b of |Yᵦ − mᵦ(X)| / aᵦ(X), with every fitted scale strictly positive. Prove s ≤ q exactly when every bin falls in the closed band mᵦ(X) ± q aᵦ(X). The repository ranks larger conformity scores as better, so apply its rule to −s. Prove the calibration-only order statistic agrees with that rule at the observed candidate, including ties. With k = floor(α(G+1)), k = 0 accepts the full space. For k > 0 the nonconformity threshold is calibration order statistic G+1−k, counted from one.
2. **Lift the rank theorem to the sampling law.** Show that per-unit scoring commutes with permuting whole conversations. For real-valued features, integrate the pointwise finite-permutation average in `coverage_orderings` under an invariant probability measure, assuming measurable scores and acceptance events. A fixed training fit suffices first. Allow a random frozen fit by joint invariance when the permutation fixes the fit. The existing `split_coverage` supplies the finite-alphabet check. The conclusion for the new unit is acceptance with probability at least 1−k/(G+1).
3. **Identify the covered event.** A maximum over turns gives a band covering every evaluated turn and bin of the new conversation simultaneously. A uniformly drawn turn per conversation gives simultaneous bin coverage for that sampled turn. Allow arbitrary dependence among turns. For a new-speaker application, identify the speaker-level object that must travel under permutations, including the treatment of recordings shared by speakers.
4. **Refute frame-level calibration by duplication.** Independently draw one fair bit per conversation and repeat it as the nonconformity score on all 20 frames. Treat one conversation's frames as 20 calibration units at α = 0.05. Derive a failure probability of 1/4 on a new independent conversation. With the proper G = 1 group rank the set is the whole space. Also recover G = 19 as the first count allowing an exclusion at that level.

**Acceptance.** Verify band membership, the measurable-law lift, both group events and the exact finite witness. Name the training and masking protocol in the resulting coverage statement. The existing density-grid group implementation supplies the group semantics. The proposed continuous spectral band has its own observable.
