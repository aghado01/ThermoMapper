# Numerical probes for conformal Bayes on conversation audio

Companion to the [five Lean briefs](README.md), posed 2026-11-16. Start with the conversation-band and ambiguity probes together. They answer the audio question directly. The remaining probes test three deployment assumptions with small, controlled experiments. The [conformal layer report](../../../conformal-layer-addendum-brief.md) records existing qualifications for grouped coverage, covariate shift, risk and alarms. Reuse the public operators and those receipts for generic checks.

## 1. Does an extra view sharpen a held-back spectrum at the same coverage target?

**Exact rank control.** At α = 0.05 give a conversation one fair-bit nonconformity score and repeat it on 20 frames. Enumerate the four equally likely calibration/test bit pairs. Ranking frames from one calibration conversation gives one miss in four. Ranking that conversation once accepts every value. Check k = 0 at G = 18 and k = 1 at G = 19 with the same tie convention as the engine. Use [SplitConformal.Calibrate](../../../../../src/maths/regression/conformal/SplitConformal.cs) on the negatives of nonconformity scores.

**Matched audio comparison.** Generate independent conversation IDs. Share a source or channel parameter across turns within each conversation and vary the number of turns. Include two-event signals spanning a quiet gap and a resonance or harmonic control from the [retained pilot source](../../../receipts/latent-recovery/probe/Synthesizer.cs). Give one predictor short-window powers, and a second those powers plus a prespecified longer-window view. Predict the same third view, measured on the same waveform and frequency grid in both arms. Register windows, hop, retained bins, taper, log offset, gap lengths and noise levels. Include gaps that the added window can span and gaps beyond its effective support. Keep the held-back array and the simulator's latent code out of the predictors' inputs. A bin from the target array copied into the added view would change the question.

Fit on training conversations only. Use a frozen BASS predictive center and positive scale for the audio comparison. A simple predictor with a fixed positive scale is an oracle for the scoring pipeline. Report the score as the maximum standardized error across target bins. Calibrate separately for each feature set at the same α. Use the same partition, drawn turn, simulated waveforms and noise in both arms. Run both `OneCase` (one sampled turn per group) and `WorstCase` (maximum over the group's turns), preserving the [engine's group semantics](../../../../../src/maths/regression/conformal/GroupConformal.cs).

Report the two distinct test events: coverage of all bins in the sampled turn, and coverage of all bins in all turns of the new conversation, each against its own calibration. Give paired changes in band width in target units, full-space frequency, calibration group count, and results by gap and conversation length. For the primary comparison use enough groups for finite thresholds, e.g. G = 99. Coverage by gap is a diagnostic unless that stratum was itself calibrated. Independent test groups can measure performance conditional on a calibration. Repeat whole calibration/test splits to assess the marginal guarantee. Width may improve, tie or worsen; record the result in each geometry. [Lean slice 1](01-conversation-band-brief.md)

## 2. Can observed set size distinguish an information limit from poor fitting?

Before fitting the audio models in probe 1, compute the raw DFT of (1,0,1) and (1,0,−1). Their short views agree. The hidden DC powers are 4 and 0, while a separately visible frequency-one power of the same unnormalized three-point DFT is 1 and 3. Under equal sign probabilities, enumerate both targets and the predictor's randomness. Returning both targets with probability 0.9 and the fixed singleton {0} otherwise gives marginal coverage 0.95 and mean accepted cardinality 1.9. With the distinguishing bin visible, an oracle singleton has coverage one and cardinality one. Check the DFT directly against those values.

Use this as a reference when reading the noisy-gap results from probe 1. Keep accepted cardinality on the two-point alphabet separate from continuous band width. Report both oracle performance and fitted performance on the same inputs so that finite calibration, regression error and physical ambiguity can be distinguished. [Lean slice 2](02-ambiguity-and-set-size-brief.md)

## 3. When does weighting rescue synthetic latent coverage?

Use two observable strata H identified by the common amplitude scale of the two impulses. Take P(H=1) = 0.05 in synthetic calibration and Q(H=1) = 0.5 in the target. Let the relative sign be fair in H=1 and positive in H=0 in both populations. Use a frozen score that makes a negative sign less conforming. Compare unweighted and exact-weighted prediction for the same target signs, including the candidate weight. The true weights for H=1 and H=0 are 10 and 10/19. At α = 0.1 compare the computed decisions to an independent small-count enumeration over calibration outcomes and target type. Report marginal and stratum miss rates, accepted cardinality, effective sample size and full-space frequency.

Then hold the observed short views fixed and set the sign always positive in the source and always negative in the target. For a transparent regime such as G = 99 and α = 0.1, verify that the calibrated singleton can miss every target with all weights equal to one. This separates failure of the conditional-law assumption from error in estimating weights. [Lean slice 3](03-latent-transport-brief.md) · [Weight contract](../../../../../src/maths/regression/conformal/WeightedConformal.cs)

## 4. Which early starts does the selected delay prevent?

Start from timestamped synthetic turns with known completions. Generate a proposed start from observable prefix information, then apply an ordered delay grid ending in abstention. Calculate a conversation-by-policy loss matrix over a fixed eligible-turn denominator, including the empty-conversation convention. Pass the matrix to [RiskControl.Mean](../../../../../src/maths/regression/conformal/LossBound.cs) and independently recompute the first corrected feasible threshold or final-policy fallback. Exercise both paths.

Report expected eligible-turn interruption fraction across repeated calibration/test conversations, start rate, and delay among starts. Include the two-turn witness where the eligible-turn loss stays at 1/2 but the early fraction among starts rises from 1/2 to 1 after an on-time start becomes an abstention. Use labeled recordings for a descriptive replay if available. Keep recorded completion times fixed when comparing policies. [Lean slice 4](04-recorded-turn-risk-brief.md)

## 5. Does the alarm have the claimed false-alarm interpretation?

Feed one frozen score per conversation into the [sequential conformal monitor](../../../../../src/maths/regression/conformal/ConformalTestMartingale.cs). On independent null streams include discrete tied scores and an all-ties control with fresh randomization. Register the bet, horizon and Ville threshold before running. For example use 200 conversations per stream, threshold 20, and 5,000 null streams. Count the fraction of streams that *ever* alarm by the horizon and give its binomial uncertainty against the 0.05 bound. A conservative alarm may be far below 0.05. Use a separate run with a prespecified change in noise level or channel response. Report pre-change alarms, post-change detection delay and censored runs.

For a negative control bypass the rank producer and feed the same uniform p-value twice into a capital calculation with bet 2·1{p ≤ 1/2}. Enumerating the two equiprobable halves gives capital four with probability 1/2. This locates the failure at the input to the alarm. [Lean slice 5](05-conversation-alarm-brief.md)

### Probes 1 and 2, registered together

Execute all four fair-bit rank cases, the G = 18/19 boundary, raw three-point DFT powers and the exact two-target randomized-set enumeration before audio fitting. Fourier tolerance is 10⁻¹⁰; rank and finite-probability assertions must match exactly up to 10⁻¹². The 0.9 both-target / 0.1 fixed-{0} rule has coverage 0.95 and size 1.9; the distinguishing-bin oracle has size one and coverage one. Accepted alphabet cardinality and continuous log-power band width have different units.

Audio has four separately declared fixed-geometry populations: two impulses at separations 64,192,384 samples in a 1,024-sample frame at 8 kHz, and a retained-pilot k = 2 resonance control padded centrally from 512 samples. These are separate experiments chosen before generation; there is no hidden-gap selector applied to a mixed deployment stream. Within a conversation share amplitude uniform on [0.6,1.4], a noise-SD choice 0.005/0.025, and resonance parameters when applicable. Draw conversation length uniformly from 1,4,12. Each turn gets gain uniform on [0.9,1.1], independent fair relative impulse sign, centre jitter uniform on integers −8..8, and independent white Gaussian waveform noise. The second impulse has amplitude 0.8 times the first. All arrays are paired between feature arms.

The short view uses length-64 periodic unit-energy Hann frames at hop 32 over the full signal, at 0,500,1500 Hz. For each frequency retain log(mean frame power + 10⁻⁴), its power-weighted normalized time centroid and time spread (denominator floor 10⁻¹²). The added view is a centred length-256 Hann transform at 125,375,875 Hz, retaining log powers with offset 10⁻⁴. The target is the length-1,024 Hann transform at 0,187.5,437.5,937.5 Hz, using the same offset and the same noisy waveform in both arms. No target array value, waveform, sign, jitter, latent code, noise level or conversation label enters regression. The target frequency is a declared prediction location. Separations 64 and 192 fit within the added window; separation 384 lies outside it even under jitter. Noisy overlapping short views are not assumed to inherit the exact noiseless DFT collision.

For each geometry generate 160 independent training conversations. Fit each feature arm separately using scalar BASS on turn/bin rows, including target frequency as an output-location predictor: two chains, burn-in 300, samples 300, thin 2, maximum basis 20 and interaction 2. Use 64 deterministically spaced pooled retained draws, predictive-noise simulation disabled, for the frozen predictive mean. A positive scale fixed from those draws' RMS noise is shared over bins and cases, with floor 10⁻⁶. Save training data fingerprints, fit settings, draw indices, diagnostics, prediction hashes and timing. No calibration outcome changes fit selection or scale. A constant training-bin mean with scale one is a feature-only scoring-pipeline oracle/baseline in both arms. It is not asserted to be the optimal noisy-audio predictor. The exact information-limit and singleton oracle are the separate two-point controls above; finite noisy fitted widths alone cannot identify an information-theoretic lower bound.

Conditional on each frozen fit, repeat 20 independent whole calibration/test splits with 99 calibration and 100 test conversations each, sampled from that geometry's same joint length/content/noise law. Independent per-conversation uniform turn marks are shared by both feature arms and the baseline. Score each turn by maximum absolute standardized error across the four target bins. Calibrate negatives of the marked-turn scores for OneCase and negatives of the maximum turn score for WorstCase with public SplitConformal.Calibrate. Verify every reported acceptance against the public GroupConformal operator using those same group events; OneCase receives the already marked single turn per calibration group. Report marked-turn coverage under OneCase and all-turn coverage under WorstCase; also retain cross-event diagnostics. Each geometry is calibrated separately; length/noise subgroup coverage is diagnostic. Record per-split and conversation outcomes, width in target log-power units, full-space frequency, RMSE and paired added-minus-short width. Report mean changes and coverage with split-cluster standard errors/95% normal intervals; 20 calibration splits limit Monte Carlo precision. Width changes have no preregistered preferred sign.

### Probe 3

At G = 99 and α = 0.1 enumerate every count triple of source types H0/positive, H1/positive, H1/negative with probabilities 0.95,0.025,0.025. Score signs +/− by 0/−1. Target type probabilities are 0.5,0.25,0.25. Compare public split-density rank decisions with a separate integer-weight enumeration. Candidate weights are included. True weights 10/19 and 10 can be scaled to exact integers 10 and 190 without changing the mathematical rule; use this exact common scaling for the primary arithmetic control. Also record literal floating true-weight decisions, including any exact-boundary differences, preserving the original gate and summing their probability mass separately. Report exact marginal/stratum misses, cardinality, both-sign/full-alphabet frequency and calibration ESS. For the failed-conditional-law control, source signs are all positive, target signs all negative and all weights are one; observed short views remain the same.

### Probe 4

Generate observable prefix times P uniform on [0.5,1.5], an observable maximum-wait budget uniform on [0.45,0.55], and completion P + 0.1 + U_c·0.6 + U_t·0.05, with a shared independent U_c per conversation and U_t per turn. Conversations are empty with probability 0.1, otherwise have 1..8 eligible turns uniformly. A delay policy proposes P + 0.1 + delay and abstains if its wait exceeds the observed budget; append an all-abstain policy. Reference completions remain fixed across policies. Evaluate 200 independent splits of 99 calibration and 500 test conversations at α = 0.1, including empty conversations with loss zero. Check every selected policy against the independently recomputed corrected mean rule, monotonicity, all-abstain loss and the two-turn denominator witness. Exercise a feasible equality case and a G = 1 all-abstain fallback where the correction prevents any feasible threshold. Report equal-conversation expected eligible-turn loss, start fraction and pooled delay among starts; Monte Carlo SE uses the 200 split-level means. Search project audio/data locations for labelled recordings; an absent labelled population remains outside the synthetic claim.
