# Slice 3 — when synthetic latent coverage transfers to real audio

**Question.** Ground truth for the latent Z is available in simulation. What assumption on real audio permits a set calibrated with those labels to cover real Z? Use a latent with the same definition in both domains. A relative sign across a gap is one example. Choose a single deployment unit as in [slice 1](01-conversation-band-brief.md) and let X contain the observations available for that unit.

1. **Change of law.** Write the synthetic joint law as Pₓ(dx) K(x,dz) and the real joint law as Qₓ(dx) K(x,dz), with a common conditional law K. Assume Qₓ is absolutely continuous with respect to Pₓ and give its true nonnegative density w, integrating to one under Pₓ. Prove the joint change-of-measure formula E_real[f(X,Z)] = E_synthetic[w(X) f(X,Z)] for bounded measurable f. Finite sums give an exact control case.
2. **Weighted coverage.** Given a fixed fit, take G independent synthetic calibration pairs and an independent real test pair. Express their pooled law as the product of G+1 synthetic laws tilted by the test coordinate's w(X). Average over candidate positions and apply `coverage_weighted` to the equivariant conformity scores. Include the test candidate's weight in the normalization, allow ties and zero calibration weights, and prove the denominator is positive almost surely under the target experiment. Conclude target miscoverage at most α for Z. The weighting unit is the entire ranked object.
3. **Counterexample with no input shift.** Reuse the collision from [slice 2](02-ambiguity-and-set-size-brief.md). Give all source mass to (1,0,1) and all target mass to (1,0,−1). Use the short views for X and the relative sign for Z. Prove the input laws coincide and the singleton {+1} covers with probability one in the source and zero in the target. The input weights are identically one. This isolates failure of the conditional-latent-law premise even when the input density ratio is known perfectly.

**Acceptance.** Verify the joint tilt, the weighted coverage corollary and the failed transfer under equal input laws. Distinguish exact weighting from any empirical ratio estimate, and keep the common conditional law explicit in the public theorem. It is an assumption about the simulator and deployment population.
