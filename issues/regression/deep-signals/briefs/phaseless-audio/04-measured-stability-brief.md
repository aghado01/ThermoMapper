# Slice 4 — conditioning in the recorded powers and log powers

**Question.** Can a weak connection between two pieces of audio explain a large reconstruction error under a small perturbation of the *recorded* cells? Use [slice 2](02-weighted-cut-brief.md) for edge products and [slice 3](03-finite-window-identifiability-brief.md) for the actual finite power map P. The [Grohs–Rathmair source](README.md) gives a continuous theorem for Gaussian Gabor magnitudes. This assignment uses finite Euclidean norms.

1. **Necessity from a sign flip.** With x = u+v and y = u−v on disjoint supports, compute the exact squared measured discrepancy, 16 Σⱼ Re((Aⱼu) conj(Aⱼv))², and the squared sign-quotient distance, 4 min(‖u‖₂², ‖v‖₂²). Every such pair in the chosen class supplies an upper bound on the best lower stability constant. Exhibit an exact or limiting weak cut. A small cut can disprove a proposed uniform guarantee. A lower stability bound requires control over every admissible pair.
2. **When a graph certifies measured stability.** State and prove a transfer lemma. For instance, if a fixed linear map B extracts the chosen edge products from the raw power vector, then ‖Δq‖₂ ≤ ‖B‖ ‖ΔP‖₂. Combine this with slice 2 for fixed amplitudes and instantiate B on the length-two windows. For a proposed Hann graph, produce a valid extraction or domination bound, or exhibit an obstruction in a stated geometry. A retained cell measuring only DC on three unit-weight samples is a simple test: two sign patterns can give the same DC power and different adjacent products. State exactly which graph, weights and operator a certificate covers.
3. **Amplitude variation and the log feature.** On a stated real signal class C use d± from slice 2 and the infimum of ‖P(x)−P(y)‖₂ / d±(x,y) over inequivalent pairs. Show why a scale-free positive bound fails on a class containing 0 and arbitrarily small multiples of a visible waveform. Work out a positive control allowing varying amplitudes, e.g. the singleton power measurement on a real scalar with |x| ≥ a > 0. On a class with every cell power in [0,U], prove the two Lipschitz bounds for log(P + ε) with constants 1/(U+ε) and 1/ε, and carry them through the data norm. If a positive lower bound κ holds in that norm, derive the waveform error bound for an observation with noise at most η and a fitted candidate with residual at most ρ.

**Done when** the cut witness, conditional transfer, scale obstruction, positive control and perturbation bound have statements with explicit norms and hypotheses. Report the strongest claim justified for the sampled pilot, even if it is a failure of stability on the unrestricted waveform space. A clustering statistic becomes a certificate only for a geometry where the comparison has been proved.
