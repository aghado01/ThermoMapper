### What a context tree learns

Imagine encoding each instant of a two-speaker conversation as `A alone`, `B alone`, `overlap`, or `silence`. Suppose the current symbol is `silence`. Does it matter who spoke immediately before the silence? Does the symbol before *that* add anything? The tree lets the answer depend on the history. One branch can stop after one symbol while another reads three. Its output is a probability distribution for the next symbol. [Kontoyiannis et al., local journal PDF](D:/aghado01/codex-scientiae/supellex-private/PDFs/Bayesian/Kontoyiannis2022.pdf)

The central calculation is small enough to see why this class is attractive. At a history \(s\), let \(E_s\) be the integrated likelihood from pooling all its next-symbol counts under one Dirichlet–categorical model. With prior stopping probability \(\beta\), the context-tree weighting recursion is

\[
W_s=\beta E_s+(1-\beta)\prod_a W_{sa},
\]

where each child looks one symbol further into the past. At maximum depth, \(W_s=E_s\). The root gives the **exact evidence averaged over all permitted trees and their transition probabilities**. Within that same recursion, \(\beta E_s/W_s\) is the posterior probability of stopping at \(s\), conditional on reaching it. You can therefore ask *how much evidence supports remembering the previous speaker after a silence?* and retain the uncertainty in the answer. [Foundational paper, §§3.1–3.4](D:/aghado01/codex-scientiae/supellex-private/gauntlet/arboreal-bayes/2007.14900v3/2007.14900v3.pdf) · [Posterior representations, §3](D:/aghado01/codex-scientiae/supellex-private/gauntlet/arboreal-bayes/2202.02239v4/2202.02239v4.pdf)

### Where the forests fit

**BART answers a different audio question:** given pause duration, pitch slope, energy decay and recent speaker activity, what is the expected next measurement? Each regression tree partitions that predictor space, and the model adds the contributions of many regularized trees. BART and BASS could therefore be compared on the **same held-back continuous view**, using the same conversations and output units. A context tree contributes something more specific: an explicit account of which suffixes of an ordered history are predictively distinct. [Chipman, George & McCulloch, BART](D:/aghado01/codex-scientiae/supellex-private/gauntlet/arboreal-bayes/0806.3286v2/0806.3286v2.pdf)

Of the forest follow-ups, **GP-BART** is pertinent if the response varies smoothly within a partition. The **BART and Bayesian CART mixing papers** matter when choosing a search algorithm: they exhibit settings in which local proposals struggle to reach a useful deep split. Their assumptions matter, especially the simplified single-tree setting of the BART lower bound. **Bayesian causal forests** become pertinent if the question changes from predicting when a speaker starts to estimating what would happen *if the system spoke earlier*. That changes what the data must identify. [GP-BART](D:/aghado01/codex-scientiae/supellex-private/gauntlet/arboreal-bayes/2204.02112v4/2204.02112v4.pdf) · [BART mixing lower bound](D:/aghado01/codex-scientiae/supellex-private/gauntlet/arboreal-bayes/2210.09352v1/2210.09352v1.pdf) · [Bayesian CART mixing](D:/aghado01/codex-scientiae/supellex-private/gauntlet/arboreal-bayes/2306.00126v1/2306.00126v1.pdf) · [Bayesian causal forests](D:/aghado01/codex-scientiae/supellex-private/gauntlet/arboreal-bayes/1706.09523v3/1706.09523v3.pdf)

Two choices seem especially consequential for future implementation. **First, choose the clock.** A suffix of three 10-ms frames means 30 ms. A suffix of three turn or acoustic events can span seconds, and its symbols alone may discard the duration of the silence you want to predict from. **Second, decide what the leaves share.** Exact tree averaging works because, for a fixed tree, the likelihood factors into leaf contributions with independently integrated parameters. A speaker effect shared across leaves can preserve that factorization *conditional on the effect*; integrating it out generally couples the leaves. That distinction matters if you eventually put a ThermoMapper regression model inside a context tree. [State-space model and factorization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.00913) · [ThermoMapper audio approach](D:/aghado01/ThermoMapper/issues/regression/deep-signals/briefs/approach-brief.md)

For our next reading, I’d take **one pause in one conversation** and work through two predictions side by side: *who speaks next?* using the discrete tree, and *what is the next measured energy?* using the state-space tree. That exposes the modeling choices before any engine design is needed.
