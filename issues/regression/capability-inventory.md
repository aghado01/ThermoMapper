## BARS

`bars/` realizes a knot configuration as a linear model and scores it by an `IObservationModel`; the chain is over (k, ξ) with coefficients and σ² integrated out.

1. **Marginal likelihood.** `IObservationModel.LogMarginalLikelihood` returns ln p(y | design) with coefficients and scale out, up to a constant depending on m alone (`bars/IObservationModel.cs:20-24`). The Normal model is exact under the unit-information g-prior at τ = 1/m, with σ² under IG(10⁻³, 10⁻³·s²) scaled to the response by default, complete, or under Jeffreys with the σ-constant omitted (`bars/WeightedNormalModel.cs:12-19, 118-134, 242-258`). Poisson and Whittle are Laplace marginals under a g-prior (`bars/PoissonModel.cs:13-21`, `bars/WhittleModel.cs:14`); Gibbs loss is the pseudo-marginal −Σρ − (ν/2)ln m (`bars/GibbsLossModel.cs:62`). Nothing is given σ²: every route integrates it (U2).
2. **Weights, offset, a changing response.** The Normal model takes weights at τ = 1/m (`bars/WeightedNormalModel.cs:254-256`); Poisson says its weights are ignored (`bars/PoissonModel.cs:21`), and Whittle and Gibbs loss never read them (`bars/WhittleModel.cs:38`, `bars/GibbsLossModel.cs:42, 69-72`). `SplineTarget` accepts weights (`bars/SplineTarget.cs:45-47`), but `Bars.Run` and `BarsConfig` carry none (`bars/Bars.cs:13-94`), so they enter only from a resampler: the weight block redraws them and swaps the target (`bars/BarsEnsemble.cs:801-826`, `ReplaceTarget` at 821). y is fixed per `Run` (`bars/BarsEnsemble.cs:489`). There is no offset (U14; E1's BARS side needs one). The Normal block reads y for its centre and for s² at every evaluation (`bars/WeightedNormalModel.cs:138-152`), so a moving y moves the prior (U3).
3. **Tempering.** `CanTemperLikelihood` and `CanTemperToPrior` declare it (`bars/IObservationModel.cs:35-41`); the ensemble refuses an unsupported plan at construction (`bars/BarsEnsemble.cs:276-293`).
   - *Likelihood path.* Normal integrated: any b in [0, 1] under a proper σ² prior, b > 0 under Jeffreys, unweighted only (`bars/WeightedNormalModel.cs:288-299`); linear form b·ℓ₁ for every model (`bars/ITemperedMarginal.cs:93-117`; Poisson, Whittle, Gibbs at `bars/PoissonModel.cs:83-86` and the like).
   - *Powered path.* Serves every model (`bars/SplineTarget.cs:88-92`).
   - *b = 0.* An exact reference draw on both paths: k ~ Poisson(1) with ordered uniforms on the powered path, k from the tabulated count law on the likelihood path (`bars/SplineTarget.cs:97-103, 148-205`).
   - *Weighted.* The weighted block exists at one τ and b = 1 (`gprior/CollapsedGPrior.cs:57-58, 310-311`), and the ensemble refuses tempering with a resampler (`bars/BarsEnsemble.cs:276`) (U1).
   - *Slope in b.* The linear form returns ℓ₁ (`bars/ITemperedMarginal.cs:111-116`). The integrated `NormalMarginal` overrides nothing, so it inherits the refusing default (`bars/ITemperedMarginal.cs:43-44`, `bars/WeightedNormalModel.cs:271-308`) although `GPriorMarginal.LogMarginalSlope` exists for the unweighted block (`gprior/CollapsedGPrior.cs:432-447`). Path sampling is therefore linear-form only (`bars/BarsEnsemble.cs:595`) (U9).
4. **Exact prior draws.** The knot measure is drawn exactly, restricted to the support by rejection (`bars/SplineTarget.cs:97-103`). (β, σ²) given knots at b = 0 would come from `GPriorConditional` at b = 0, whose domain allows it (`gprior/CollapsedGPrior.cs:267-271`), but no BARS method assembles it (U13). The σ² scale and the centre read y, so the prior is the resolved one (`bars/WeightedNormalModel.cs:98-103`).
5. **Conditional draws.** Normal: σ² ~ IG, then β through the band factor, exact (`bars/WeightedNormalModel.cs:154-164`; `gprior/CollapsedGPrior.cs:514-528`). Poisson: an independence Metropolis–Hastings of five iterations from a Laplace proposal (`bars/PoissonModel.cs:145-180`), Whittle a Laplace draw, both labelled `Approximate`; Gibbs loss returns the M-estimate, `KnotOnly` (`bars/CoefficientUncertainty.cs:8-20`, `bars/GibbsLossModel.cs:93-95`). The labels are the declaration E33 asks for, and BARS is the only family with such a label (U21).
6. **Derivatives, smoothness.** Degree 3 by default (`bars/Bars.cs:16`), so a draw is C² at the simple knots, the strictly ordered interior knots (`bars/SplineTarget.cs:112`); `StepBasis` is piecewise constant (`bars/StepBasis.cs:7-14`). `IBasis` has `Dimension`, `Design` and `Evaluate` only (`spline/IBasis.cs:7-23`), and `BSpline` has the basis and the clamped knots (`spline/BSpline.cs:12, 57`), so there is no derivative of a fit. `SplineExtrema` recovers each span's polynomial from evaluations and solves for derivative roots, giving each draw's exact argmax and peak spans (`bars/SplineExtrema.cs:17-18, 209-211`; `bars/BarsEnsemble.cs:864-870`) (U11, U17).
7. **Predictive draws, pointwise.** `PointwiseLogDensity` has two contents (`bars/BarsEnsemble.cs:626-715`). Retained parameters: Normal (with the resampler's marginal replacing N(y; zβ, σ²)), Poisson and Whittle; Gibbs loss refuses, its likelihood being a pseudo-likelihood (`bars/BarsEnsemble.cs:639-648`). Collapsed predictive: Normal without a resampler only. No new-y draw exists; the result keeps each draw's knots, coefficients and σ² (`bars/BarsEnsemble.cs:858-859`) (U10, N3).
8. **Resume.** `Run(checkpoint, resumeFrom)`, `RestoreChain` and `Save` carry the chain or ladder, every stream, the kernel's τ, the weights and the readouts (`bars/BarsEnsemble.cs:416-475`). The scan's `copy` is an identity because the run is its own state (`bars/BarsEnsemble.cs:393, 442-444`). `BarsResumeTests.cs:36-54` runs six setups (plain, robust, adaptive, tempered, reference-powered, reference-linear) and compares fit, draws, peaks, move statistics and the tempering readout; it does not compare `Evidence`, and no setup is the integrated form with a likelihood-path reference rung, the default that reports an evidence.
9. **Evidence.** `ReportsEvidence` needs the `evidence` option (on by default), a tempered ladder, the reference rung, the likelihood path and a `WeightedNormalModel` with a proper σ² prior (`bars/BarsEnsemble.cs:324-325`; `bars/WeightedNormalModel.cs:90`). The count prior needs no normalizing: it is "up to an additive constant" (`bars/ComplexityPriors.cs:9-10`) and the reference rung normalizes it by tabulation (`bars/SplineTarget.cs:154-181`), so the ratio is the evidence under the restricted, normalized knot prior (my reading of the two). The prior's restrictions are declared in `BarsEvidencePriors` (`bars/BarsEnsemble.cs:588-597`). Stepping-stone always; path sampling needs the slope, which only the linear form supplies. Poisson and Whittle drop a known constant (`bars/PoissonModel.cs:20-21`) and report none (U16); Gibbs loss has no likelihood to normalize (N3).

## BAPS

`baps/Baps.cs` fits a P-spline on a caller-supplied banded design, with four schemes: Gibbs, collapsed (Metropolis on log λ), locally adaptive and anisotropic (`baps/Baps.cs:14-51`).

1. **Marginal likelihood.** Only the collapsed scheme has one: the profiled REML evidence, β integrated under the improper penalty prior with σ² under Jeffreys, up to a λ-independent constant (`baps/PenalizedSpline.cs:90-105`; `baps/Baps.cs:283-284`), plus the log edf-slope prior that makes the λ target proper (`baps/Baps.cs:338-341`). It ranks λ values; it is not an evidence (N1).
2. **Weights, offset, a changing response.** None. The constructor fixes y and accumulates ZᵀZ and Zᵀy once (`baps/Baps.cs:176-177, 210-225`). `BandedDesign.Accumulate` already takes weights (`baps/Baps.cs:225` passes null; `bars/GibbsLossModel.cs:117` passes some), so a weighted Gram is a call away (U18).
3. **Tempering.** None: no inverse-temperature argument exists in `Baps`, `HierarchicalPSpline` or `PenalizedSpline`. The Gibbs state includes β and σ², so the tempered target b·ℓ₁(β, σ²) is separable with slope ℓ₁ and conjugate conditionals (σ²'s shape gains bn/2, β's precision becomes bZᵀZ + λP) (U12, my reading, untested). A b = 0 member is the improper prior as stated (N1); a proper prior gives one (U20).
4. **Exact prior draws.** `IBandPenalty.SamplePrior` draws the penalised part and leaves the null space out, because its prior is flat (`baps/IBandPenalty.cs:68-70`; `baps/SmoothingVariancePriors.cs:125-128, 152`; `baps/TensorPenalty.cs:58`). The model's own prior is improper there (`baps/PenalizedSpline.cs:12-15`), and σ² may be Jeffreys (`baps/NoiseVariancePrior.cs`) (N2).
5. **Conditional draws.** Gibbs: β banded Gaussian, σ² and τ² inverse gamma, η gamma (`baps/Baps.cs:521-625`); anisotropic τⱼ² by slice or Newton–Metropolis (`baps/Baps.cs:602-611`); collapsed: log λ by random-walk Metropolis, then σ² and β exactly given λ (`baps/Baps.cs:22-26, 630-649`).
6. **Derivatives, smoothness.** The fit is Zβ over the caller's design (`baps/Baps.cs:109-121`); `FitBand` evaluates any rows of the same basis. Smoothness is the basis's (a cubic B-spline is C²) and the penalty's order; no derivative design exists to pass in (U11, U17).
7. **Predictive, pointwise.** Retained parameters only, N(yᵢ; zᵢβ, σ²) (`baps/Baps.cs:660-696`). No predictive draw (U10).
8. **Resume.** `Run(checkpoint, resumeFrom)`, `RestoreChain` (`baps/Baps.cs:269-271, 388-389`); the scan copies its state for real (`baps/Baps.cs:370-371`). Tested for each scheme (`BapsEnsembleTests.cs:43`).
9. **Evidence.** None under the stated prior, for the reason at row 1 and row 3 (N1); a proper prior gives one (U20).

## Hierarchical P-spline

`baps/HierarchicalPSpline.cs`: replicate curves β_r around a penalised population curve β₀, five Gibbs schemes (centred, collapsed population, interweaved, both, parameter-expanded half-t) (`baps/HierarchicalPSpline.cs:141-166, 177-197`). It is BAPS's family for every row but these:
- *Responses* are `double[][]`, fixed with Zᵀy_r at construction (`baps/HierarchicalPSpline.cs:200-212, 244-248`) (U18). The half-t scale defaults to the responses' pooled sd (`baps/HierarchicalPSpline.cs:175`), a prior reading y (U3).
- *Pointwise.* A unit holding all of replicate r integrates β_r out (the question of a new curve); a unit holding some of it conditions on β_r (`baps/HierarchicalPSpline.cs:430-441`). There the unit map decides which latent is integrated: a replicate's curve is a group latent, and the two readings answer a new curve and a new point on a seen one.
- *Resume.* Tested for every scheme (`HierarchicalEnsembleTests.cs:141, 169`); real state copy at `baps/HierarchicalPSpline.cs:577, 600`.

## BGP

`bgp/GpRegression.cs` is the conjugate core; `bgp/BgpSampler.cs` samples the bandwidth t (and the noise ratio η) by Metropolis on log t under an empirical-Bayes prior (Tang, Wu, Cheng and Dunson 2025).

1. **Marginal likelihood.** Two modes (`bgp/GpRegression.cs:8-23`). *Known:* σ² given, signal variance 1, mean integrated under a flat prior, ln p(Y | t) (`bgp/GpRegression.cs:159-168, 235-237`). *Unknown scale:* s² integrated under 1/s², leaving ln p(Y | t, η) (`bgp/GpRegression.cs:170-176, 237-238`). This is the one family that has a marginal given σ², but s² is forced to 1 there; σ² given with s² free has no mode (U7).
2. **Weights, offset, a moving response.** `_y` is the training response less its mean, set in the constructor (`bgp/GpRegression.cs:58-60, 110-111`); the pairwise squared distances do not read it (`bgp/GpRegression.cs:64-65, 114-122`), so a y per fit is cheap. The nugget is one scalar (`bgp/GpRegression.cs:199-204`): no weights, no per-observation noise. The mean is integrated, so there is no declared mean or offset (U7).
3. **Tempering.** None. A power on the likelihood would give the same Gaussian marginal with nugget σ²/b and a b-dependent prefactor, so a closed form exists (my reading, untested) (U12).
4. **Exact prior draws.** None. The bandwidth prior drops its normalizer and reads the inputs' k-NN distances (`bgp/EmpiricalBayesBandwidthPrior.cs:7-18, 55-69`); it is a density on a bounded interval (γ₁T_n², 1], so it can be normalized in one dimension (U15). The mean's flat prior is improper (U15).
5. **Conditional draws.** s² | t, η ~ IG((n−1)/2, Q/2) exactly, per retained draw (`bgp/BgpSampler.cs:578`). t and η are drawn by (adaptive) Metropolis (`bgp/BgpSampler.cs:494-524`). f is integrated and never drawn; the GP gives its conditional mean and variance (`bgp/GpRegression.cs:295-363`) (U10).
6. **Derivatives, smoothness.** The squared-exponential kernel (`bgp/GpKernel.cs:21`) makes the posterior mean and every draw infinitely smooth. `IGpKernel` has `Evaluate(sqDistance, t)` only (`bgp/GpKernel.cs:13-17`), so a derivative or a gradient covariance cannot be formed (U11, U17).
7. **Predictive, pointwise.** Posterior mean and sd of f at test points by total variance (`bgp/BgpSampler.cs:56-57, 298-299`); "a new observation adds σ²" is left to the caller (`bgp/GpRegression.cs:290`). Pointwise: the leave-unit-out predictive with f, m and s² integrated, its one content, PSIS-LOO and not WAIC (`bgp/BgpSampler.cs:312-345`); while f is integrated there is no retained-parameter content (N6).
8. **Resume.** `RestoreChain` recomputes the fit at the saved (t, η) (`bgp/BgpSampler.cs:182-191`); tested (`BgpResumeTests.cs:42`).
9. **Evidence.** None: the flat mean prior and 1/s² are improper, the bandwidth prior is unnormalised, and nothing tempers. E9 names BGP among its engines (U15).

## BASS

`bass/` is the largest family: structure and τ sampled, coefficients and σ² integrated out of the structure step (D04) and composed at retention.

1. **Marginal likelihood.** `BassTarget` is the posterior over (structure, τ) with a, σ² out, under the g-form or the normalized ridge, the intercept's convention and σ²'s prior (`bass/BassTarget.cs:33-52, 129-140`). `GPriorMarginal.LogMarginal(sigmaPrior)` is the only form; none is given σ² (`gprior/CollapsedGPrior.cs:396-423`) (U2).
2. **Weights, offset, a moving response.** `WorkingResponse` carries y − offset, weights and an output inner product Q (`bass/BassObservationModel.cs:21-61`), and `IBassObservationModel` has the latent slots (`bass/BassObservationModel.cs:89-138`), but `BassDesign` refuses weights and Q ≠ I (`bass/BassDesign.cs:150-154`) (U1). `BassModel.Create` builds the working response once (`bass/BassModel.cs:44`), the design stores ỹ read-only and its mean at construction (`bass/BassDesign.cs:118, 155-161`), and `BassResolved.Resolve` reads the working values once for σ²'s scale and the level (`bass/BassResolved.cs:104-118`). Cached `BassStructureFit`s hold the residual and fitted sums (`bass/BassState.cs:19-23`), so a response that moves needs every cached fit re-evaluated (U4, U3). The pointwise content and the training-fit summary read `Data.Response`, not y − offset (`bass/BassPointwise.cs:97, 127`; `bass/presentation/BassTrainingFit.cs:52`), and prediction holds no offset to add back (U4).
3. **Tempering.**
   - *Likelihood path.* The integrated form (a, σ² integrated against the tempered likelihood) and the linear form; defined at b = 0 under the proper defaults, from b > 0 under Jeffreys or the flat intercept (`bass/BassTarget.cs:160-180, 250-275`).
   - *Powered path.* τ powered in log τ (`bass/BassTarget.cs:38-41`).
   - *b = 0.* `BassPriorSampler` draws the prior on F exactly, by rejection from the globally truncated measure (`bass/BassPriorSampler.cs:11-26, 97-105`).
   - *Slope in b.* The target supplies it on the integrated form for unweighted blocks (`bass/BassTarget.cs:259-266` over `gprior/CollapsedGPrior.cs:432-447`) and ℓ₁ on the linear form. The ladder traces slopes on the linear form only (`bass/BassLadder.cs:218`), because at b = 0 the integrated slope carries −(k/2)·E[1/τ], infinite under Zellner–Siow, and its discretization diagnostic misses E_b[∂²ℓ_b/∂b²] (`bass/BassEvidence.cs:25-29`) (U19).
   - *Others.* SMC and the flat-histogram sampler temper through the same target; WHAM's pilot needs the linear form (`bass/BassConfig.cs:215-217`).
4. **Exact prior draws.** (Structure, τ) exactly on F (`bass/BassPriorSampler.cs:97-105`). (a, σ²) are composed only at b = 1 (`bass/BassSweep.cs:181-192`); the block at b = 0 would give them (U13).
5. **Conditional draws.** `Compose`: σ² from its collapsed conditional, a given σ², and λ | M ~ Gamma(h₁ + M, h₂ + 1), each exact given (structure, τ) (`bass/BassSweep.cs:176-192`). The sweep's coefficient, latent and scale slots are empty for Gaussian noise (`bass/BassObservationModel.cs:102-113`).
6. **Derivatives, smoothness.** The design-kernel contract carries a part's derivative, one variable at a time, the mean of the one-sided limits at a kink (`bass/DesignKernel.cs:15-17, 49-52, 168-175`; `bass/BassBasis.cs:69-82, 96-105`), and `BassMoments.FactorDerivative` reads it (`bass/sensitivity/BassMoments.cs:143-147`). A categorical part has none (`bass/DesignKernels.cs:208-209`). Hinge degree q is C^{q−1}, and the default `Degree = 1` has kinks at its knots (`bass/BassConfig.cs:258`). No posterior-level derivative of f exists: nothing under `bass/posterior/` differentiates (U11, U17).
7. **Predictive, pointwise.** `BassFit.Predict` gives latent and predictive draws, a basis fit adding component noise and a truncation residual (`bass/BassFit.cs:76-84`; `bass/posterior/BassPrediction.cs:9-55`). Pointwise has the retained-parameters and collapsed-predictive contents, with a unit map that makes a curve one unit (`bass/BassPointwise.cs:9-29`).
8. **Resume.** Chains, ladders, SMC replicates and flat-histogram runs continue bit for bit (`bass/BassRun.cs:32-35`; `bass/BassSmcRun.cs:41-56`; `bass/BassFlatHistogramRun.cs:44-54`), tested (`BassRunResumeTests.cs:56, 124`; `BassTemperingResumeTests.cs:32, 85, 128`). The chain's scan passes an identity copy (`bass/BassSweep.cs:492`).
9. **Evidence.** By ladder: asked for, a reference rung, a σ² prior that is not Jeffreys, an intercept that is not flat and a proper τ prior (`bass/BassRun.cs:105-106`); by SMC that reached b = 1; or by a flat-histogram run from b_lo = 0 (`bass/BassEvidence.cs:14-21, 88-115`). Flagged for saturation and for the chains' disagreement. Under Jeffreys or the flat intercept the value omits a constant and is not reported (N5).
